## About Me

Roel Sanchez is a Senior Scientist at BASF Corporation and a Visiting Scholar at the University of California, Berkeley. He specializes in translating R&D innovation into commercial technologies at the intersection of sustainable materials, industrial chemistry, and digital transformation. His work combines scientific modeling, process development, and strategic university–industry collaborations to generate measurable customer and commercial value.

Highlights:

- Recognized by BASF leadership for advancing catalyst innovation through strategic university collaborations, contributing to commercialized technologies and co-authorship in Science.
- Trusted technical partner with a proven track record of moving research from concept to customer-ready solutions.
- Completed a Deep Learning course at New York University taught by Prof. Yann LeCun; developed a finalist-ranked PyTorch project on self-supervised object detection.
- Languages: English and Spanish (bilingual); German (intermediate, Goethe‑Zertifikat B2); French (basic).

## Research Interests

- **Sustainability** — materials and process design to reduce environmental impact and improve resource efficiency.
- **Semiconductors** — materials, device processes, and integration for next‑generation electronic and energy applications.
- **Petrochemicals** — catalyst design and process optimization for industrial chemical production.
- **Automation** — process automation, control systems, and instrumentation to improve reliability and throughput.
- **Digitalization** — machine learning, data‑driven modeling, and digital tools that accelerate R&D and scale lab results to production.
